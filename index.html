    04 / SELF-SERVICE TOOLS

    Calculate, check & prepare.

    Useful first-step tools for tax, GST and document preparation. Final filing/classification should be verified for the actual facts.

    ₹

    Income Tax Calculator

    First tell us what type of income you have. The calculator will not apply normal slab rates to special-rate income automatically.

    Estimate only. Capital gains and special-rate income require section/asset details; lottery and similar winnings are taxed under special provisions, not ordinary slabs.
    GST

    HSN & GST Rate Finder

    Search common product descriptions or enter an HSN heading to see a reference rate.

    Open official CBIC GST rate table ↗Reference tool only. Confirm the exact classification/rate on the official GST system before invoicing or filing.
    ↑

    Upload Documents

    Before sending documents, enter your details so the CA team can identify exactly who submitted each file.

    Your name, mobile, email, service and purpose are saved with the upload record. Current demo storage is browser-based; connect Firebase Storage/Firestore for CA-side access across devices.
